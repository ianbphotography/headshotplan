Common questions

“We only need headshots every few years.”

You hire every month. Headshot day is out of date by spring. The plan keeps your page current without planning another headshot day.

“Another monthly bill?”

It replaces the headshot day, the new-hire scramble and the event photographer, on one invoice. A 150-person firm pays $1,500 a month for about $27,500 of photography a year.

“What if we don’t hire anyone this quarter?”

Use a Big Shoot, or refresh anyone whose photo is getting old. Nothing goes to waste.

“People won’t show up.”

We run the day: a scheduling link, reminders, and a free make-up slot for anyone on a job site.

“Why not AI headshots?”

AI makes a picture of someone who looks like you. We make a picture of you: real light, real expression, coached in person. Your clients meet the real person. And people can tell: on LinkedIn, an AI headshot can make you look like a spam account.

“We already have a project photographer.”

Keep them for award shots of your buildings. Use your Big Shoots for events and your office.
